Mobile DAM, phase 1

The 2.1 scope: find, check and download, read-only, inside Unity's phone shell. Open this page on your phone to try it full screen.

Scenario

Mobile experience flag
Off is what phones get today: the SCR-320 holding screen.
Access level
Download needs Admin by default. View users also don't see drafts.
Folders this user can see
With one folder the Dashboard is skipped and the folder opens directly.
Folder loading
Search inside a folder
Full screen is the 2.1 plan. Inline is the earlier version, kept to compare.
AI features and file metadata
Off for FIFA. On adds File metadata to Details, plus two 2.2 features: More like this on the asset and the natural-language reading in search.
Collections
On for most tenants, off for FIFA. Off removes the Library and Collections tabs.
Rights
Off for FIFA. On adds Expired and Embargoed badges, the Rights section and download limits.
Theme
Opens in light whatever your computer is set to.
Screen
Landscape and tablet portrait reflow the phone layout: more grid columns, centred sheets.
Open a shared link
As if tapped in email, Slack or Teams. Back goes to the asset's folder.

Flows to try

  1. In Spring 2026 launch, tap search, pick "hero" from recent searches and download the Web version.
  2. Step through those results: swipe on the asset, or use ‹ › by the counter.
  3. Apply the saved filter "UK social" from the search screen, or from the top of Filters.
  4. With AI features on (2.2), in Product Packshots, search "png for uk" and take the reading at the top.
  5. Open Filters, type "uk" in Find attributes or tags and tick Market › UK. Swipe the sheet down to close it without applying.
  6. Open the 412 MB PSD in Spring 2026 launch and tap Download.
  7. Open a shared link from the panel: an asset, a collection, or one you can't access.
  8. On the Dashboard, open Launch hero set from Shared collections, then try See all 4.
  9. With Rights on: open the expired TV ad in Spring 2026 launch, the embargoed screen loop in Retail POS Toolkit, and the Night Cream and Serum Angle packshots in Product Packshots.
  10. In Product Packshots, open Filters, tick File › Image, then Attributes › Product line › Serum, and remove one from the pills at the top.
  11. In Brand Identity, type "guide" and open the guidelines PDF straight from the suggestions.
  12. In Spring 2026 launch, search "videos for social" and play the cutdown.
  13. Add the 16:9 hero and the 15-second cutdown to the queue, then save them to the phone.
  14. Add the 412 MB layered PSD to the queue and see it hand over to desktop.
  15. Tap Select, pick two social images and download them to the phone; then Select all and see the selection go to the queue.
  16. Open the Collections tab in Spring 2026 launch, open Launch hero set: search and filter it from the icons, select from it, share it or download it all.
  17. Leave for Timesheets through Unity's menu, then come back to the DAM.
  18. Open Holiday Gifting 2026, then try a search that finds nothing.
  19. Switch the flag off, then try "Copy link".
