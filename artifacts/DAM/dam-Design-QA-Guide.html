    sdDam Design QA Guide

    Six months of work moved the DAM onto the Scale design system. This is what "correct" looks like, where the risk sits, and what not to raise.

    Compare to neighboursIf one chip, header or field differs from the ones beside it, that difference is the bug.
    Short menus of valuesSizes, colours, radii, shadows and icons each come from a list of 4 to 6. "In between" is suspect.
    Every state countsHover, pressed, Tab focus, disabled, loading, empty, dark, phone. Bugs hide in states.
    Not every difference is a defectCheck Accepted, don't raise before logging.

    Words used here

    Design shorthand you'll meet in this guide and in tickets. Plain meanings only.

    • Accent / cerulean the one brand colour used for emphasis. Cerulean is the DS name; it reads as a teal-blue on screen. When a ticket says "accent", "cerulean" or "primary colour", it means this.
    • Backdrop / scrim the dimmed layer behind a panel or dialog.
    • Bento the collection tile on the dashboard that shows a small grid of thumbnails with a "+N" count cell.
    • Breakpoint a window width where the layout changes. Phone is 767px and below.
    • Bulk bar / selection tray the dark bar pinned to the bottom when you've selected several assets.
    • Chip a small rounded label, e.g. an active filter you can remove.
    • Chrome the frame around the content: nav, header, panels, toolbars. "Global chrome" is the part that stays on every page.
    • Contrast / AA how readable text is against its background. AA is the accessibility pass mark.
    • Design system (Scale) the shared rulebook of colours, sizes and components the app is built from. "DS" for short.
    • Disclosure the little triangle or chevron that opens and closes a group.
    • Empty state the message shown when a list has nothing in it: icon, title, hint, sometimes a button.
    • Eyebrow a small uppercase label above a heading or widget, e.g. RECENTLY ADDED.
    • Focus ring the outline that appears on a control when you reach it with the Tab key.
    • Gate badge the badge that says why an asset is held back: draft, restricted or expiring. Only one shows.
    • Gutter the empty margin between a thing and the edge of its container.
    • Hairline the thinnest divider line between rows.
    • Hatch overlay the diagonal-stripe wash drawn over draft thumbnails.
    • Host the legacy Screendragon app the DAM sits inside. It supplies login and the light/dark theme.
    • Kit / atom a small shared building block (button, field row, list item) reused everywhere. Two atoms of the same kind must look identical.
    • Lightbox the full-screen viewer that opens when you click an asset.
    • Mosaic the library view where images tile edge to edge at their own proportions.
    • Neutral the grey family used for text, lines and backgrounds. Ours is warm ("Sand").
    • Panel a side area that opens beside the content: Filters, Collections, the lightbox Details pane, the edit-mode tabs.
    • Pill / badge a tiny rounded tag on a card: JPG, AI, DRAFT.
    • Portaled dev term for menus, tooltips and popovers that render outside the page's main box, so they can miss the theme.
    • Pressed state how a control looks while the mouse button is held down on it.
    • Rail a narrow vertical strip of icons: the left nav when collapsed, the tab strip in the lightbox and edit modal.
    • Role (type) a size plus a weight with a job, e.g. panel header = 16 at 600. Text takes a role; it never picks its own size.
    • Scope link the text in the bulk bar that says what the selection covers ("3 of 120 in this folder").
    • Skeleton grey placeholder blocks shown while content loads, shaped like the content.
    • Stage the area inside the lightbox or editor where the image or document sits.
    • Step (1 to 12) a colour's position on its twelve-step ramp. The number tells you its job: 1–2 backgrounds, 3–5 fills, 6–8 lines, 9–10 solid, 11–12 text.
    • Surface any distinct screen or area: the dashboard, the library grid, a dialog, a panel.
    • Token a named value in that rulebook, e.g. "accent" or "card radius". You'll see them in dev replies; you never need to read them.

    Colour in full: how the palette is made and what each step means

    The app uses two families, cerulean and Sand, each generated from one seed colour into twelve steps by the design system. Every hue in the system, including the status colours, uses the same twelve roles, so a step number means the same thing everywhere: 3 is always a resting fill, 7 always a border, 11 always secondary text. Light and dark are separate twelve-step ramps with the same roles.

    The twelve steps

    The supporting hues: same twelve roles, one job each

    Eight more ramps ship in the app. None of them is an accent; each carries one meaning, and every use follows the same recipe as the status pills: fill 3, border 7, text 11, with 9 for a solid mark. Step 9 is the same value in light and dark for all of them; the surrounding steps flip.

    Reading a colour bug by its step

    Text looks too paleIt is on step 8 or 9. Text is only ever 11 or 12 (8 is reserved for disabled).
    A line is invisible or too heavyBorders are 6 to 8 alpha. A 3 disappears; a 10 reads as a bar.
    Hover jumps too farHover is one step (3 to 4, or alpha 3), pressed one more (4 to 5, or alpha 4). Two steps at once is wrong.
    A colour is right in light, wrong in darkUsually a fixed hex instead of a step, or a component rendered outside the theme.
    Two teals, or a blueOnly one accent family exists. Indigo, blue and cool grey are pre-system leftovers.
    Amber outside a document pageAmber is the highlight role only. Pending pills use amber too, but as fill 3 / text 11, never the bright 5 to 6.

    Typography in full: one face, seven sizes, four weights, thirteen roles

    Every piece of text in the app is one typeface (Inter) at one of seven ladder sizes, each with a fixed line height, in one of four weights. A "role" is a size plus a weight with a job, e.g. panel header = 16 at 600. Components take a role; they never choose a size or weight on their own.     Truncation and wrapping

    Reading a type bug

    Wrong typefaceArial, Segoe, Helvetica or a serif means Inter didn't load or the element rendered outside the app's frame (menus, tooltips). The design system's own default is the system font; the app pins Inter.
    Size off the ladder13, 15, 17, 18 or bigger inside the chrome. Often a leftover from the 12.5px era: something authored at "1.25rem" now renders 20 instead of 16.
    Wrong weightBold headings, bold active tabs, or a regular-weight button label. Check the weights card above.
    Uppercase with no trackingAn eyebrow or badge in caps with letters touching is missing its 0.08em spacing.
    Line height crushed or looseTwo-line text whose lines overlap, or a 12px label with big gaps. Each size has one line height.
    Two roles for one jobTwo panel titles at different sizes, or a section header that matches body weight. The sibling is the spec.

    Global chrome

    The frame that stays on every page: left nav, header row, global search, menus, tooltips and toasts. Check it once, then trust it while you move around.

    Dashboard

    The landing page: a title row, folder lanes, collection tiles, recent lists and the event banner. Rebuilt in August. Compare against dashboard presets.

    Library

    The asset browser, in grid, list or mosaic view, with cards you can select and open. Enter via a folder card; the Library nav item is disabled until a folder is chosen, by design. Selection behaviour: selection model.

    Filters and Collections panels

    The two side panels on the left: Filters (narrow the library) and Collections (browse saved sets). Collections was restyled to be the twin of Filters. Test them side by side.

    Lightbox

    The full-screen viewer that opens when you click an asset, with a tab rail and a side pane on the right. Reworked in September. The PDF path was never opened in a browser by the developer.

    Edit, bulk edit, upload

    The three editing screens: edit one asset, edit many at once, and the upload queue. Each has a left tab rail, a canvas and a side panel.

    These three modals are always dark, even in light mode. Judge them against each other. Tabs: edit panel destinations.

    Downloads, queue, approvals

    The Downloads page and its history tab, the configure-download panel from a card menu, the download queue button, and the table-based Approvals and admin pages.

    Phone and tablet

    Never tested on a real device. Built against a browser at 390px. Test on real iOS and Android inside the host. Phone ≤767px; tablet 768 to 1023 gets the desktop layout on purpose.

    Dark mode

    The host sets the theme; there is no in-app switch. If your host can toggle, the DAM changes without a reload.

    States sweep, once per surface

    After the surface-specific checks, run these six on the same screen. A "state" is how something looks while you hover, press, Tab to it, or while it is disabled, loading or empty.

    Where bugs hide after a design-system change

    You don't need the mechanics. Each card says what the failure looks like on screen.

    • Design-system bumps (three since July)The package ships its own spacing scale that clashes with the app's. Looks like: a field 24 or 40 tall instead of 32; gaps off the 8px rhythm; twin panels at different widths.
    • Root font size was 12.5px for a yearFixed 17 Sep; every dependent size was frozen to px. Looks like: one thing 28% bigger than its siblings. Intended growths: search to 560, panel floors to 400, list thumbs to 80.
    • 2,257 lines of "dead" CSS deleted on 21 SepLooks like: browser defaults: blue underlined links, a serif, a grey square button, list bullets, no hover, a raw-text pill over an image.
    • Routes and modals load on demandEach route's CSS arrives with its code. Looks like: something correct only after you've opened the lightbox or edit modal once. Test each route on a cold reload in a fresh tab.
    • Menus, tooltips, popovers render outside the themeLooks like: a dropdown with bigger text than the page, or a light menu on a dark app.
    • Grid columns come from the containerLooks like: a panel toggle that doesn't change the column count, cards far under 230, a right gutter.
    • Dark panels inside a light appEdit, bulk, upload are always dark. Looks like: invisible labels, near-white hairlines, a light input on the dark panel.
    • Copy comes from the databasePlaceholders and labels are generated from a locale DB. Old wording ("Search for attributes…" vs "Find attributes…") is a content hand-off, not a frontend bug.
    • Environment, not codeThe dev tenant's image vault is empty and the dev user may have no folders. Looks like: broken thumbnails, broken hero banners, a disabled Library nav item. Confirm on a populated tenant.
    • The DAM lives in the host's iframeNo URL routing: refresh returns to the dashboard. Theme comes from the host. Measure gutters from the DAM frame, not the host window.
    • PDF, OCR and mobile were never eyeballedNo local PDFs, no device. Spend extra time on multi-page documents, OCR results and phones.
    • Inter is the only fontAny other typeface means a load failure or lost style. Check on a machine that has never opened the app.

    Accepted, don't raise

    Deliberate decisions. If you disagree, log a design question, not a bug.

    • Side panels snap, no slideSo the grid re-flows instantly.
    • Edit, bulk, upload are always darkThe panel skin needs a dark base.
    • No in-app dark-mode switchThe host supplies the theme.
    • Primary buttons differ in darkLight cerulean, dark text. Contrast.
    • Scrollbar thumb grows on hover with no transitionChrome can't animate it.
    • Edit Attributes show "0 of N"; library filters hide zeroKnown; decision pending.
    • No heart toggle for FavouritesIt's a collection.
    • Duplicate check has two options"New version" removed by design.
    • No Queue in the phone headerTab bar has Downloads.
    • Tablets get the desktop layoutPending a device pass.
    • Refresh returns to the dashboardNo URL routing in the iframe.
    • Dark-panel text fields show focus as a border change; sort trigger ring fadesDesign-system questions, left open.
    • Solid buttons show a faint press haloFrom the design system.
    • Light primary contrast is just over AA (4.61:1)Logged, not this cycle.
    • Broken imagery on the dev tenantEmpty vault.
    • Lightbox and edit not phone-designedRaise breakages only.

    Reporting a design bug

    A design bug is a comparison. Name the sibling you compared to and the fix is usually one line.

    Surface:     Library › list view › row hover
Compared to: Dashboard › Recent downloads › row hover
Difference:  Library hover is darker
Expected:    Same hover tint everywhere (guide › Contract › States)
Where:       Light, 1440 wide, Chrome 153, dev tenant, 21 Sep build
Attach:      Two screenshots side by side (+ dark if relevant)
Ref:         SCR-244
    BlockerUnreadable text, invisible focus, unreachable controls, undismissable modal, sideways page scroll on phone. DefectAny contract value off-list, any sibling mismatch, any missing state, anything in Dark mode, any browser default. QuestionAnything in "Accepted" you disagree with; copy wording; anything not reproducible on a populated tenant.
