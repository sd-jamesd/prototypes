Two rules that differ from everywhere else

Every stroke carries a 2px outline. Annotations sit on assets of unknown colour, so no palette can guarantee contrast and no ratio can be quoted. The outline is white or black, chosen by the luminance under the stroke. This is what makes the hue free to be pure identity, which is what annotation colour is actually for.
Cyan is allowed here and excluded from the action picker. Teal is the brand accent, so a cyan workflow badge would read as an interactive control. An annotation sits on an image, not beside a button, so nothing competes and cyan is genuinely useful over photography. This is why the two pickers do not share a palette even though they share a dictionary.

The palette

Thirteen families at --colors-{family}-9, plus --annotation-white and --annotation-black. Step 9 is the solid-fill step: nothing sits on top of an annotation stroke, so the icon and text steps do not apply. Same reasoning as chart series.

Minimum separation across the thirteen is 15.9 in CIE76, between Purple and Plum. That is the closest pair and the one to watch if the palette ever needs trimming.

Are these all tokens

Fourteen of the sixteen are: every hue resolves to --colors-{family}-9 straight from the ramp. Two are not, and deliberately.

TokenValueWhy it sits outside the ramp
--annotation-white#ffffffSand-1 is #fdfdfc, slightly warm. Over photography a warm near-white reads cream and loses the maximum-contrast property the marker exists for.
--annotation-black#000000Sand-12 is #21201c, warm enough to read brown against an image. A marker whose only job is contrast against unknown content should be at the extreme.
--annotation-outline-light#ffffffThe 2px stroke outline, used when the content underneath is dark.
--annotation-outline-dark#000000Used when the content underneath is light. Chosen at render time from the local luminance, not authored.

Naming them matters more than which value they hold. If they stay as raw hex in the component they will drift, and if they are forced into the neutral ramp they stop doing their job. A named token outside the ramp is the honest answer, the same way the capacity triad and the chart series are named signal palettes rather than ramp applications.

One more value needs a token. The proofing canvas is #12110e, hardcoded. It is the only legitimate dark surface in the product, since it is a viewing environment rather than chrome, and it should read from a dark-mode surface token rather than a literal.
